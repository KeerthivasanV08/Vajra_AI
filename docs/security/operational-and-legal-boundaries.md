# Security — Operational & Legal Boundaries

> **Platform**: VAJRA AI Predictive Cybercrime Interception Platform

---

## 1. Prototype Policy & Decision Support

All SOP action tiers (`MONITOR`, `SOFT_ALERT`, `RECOMMEND_HOLD`, `ESCALATE_FREEZE`, `INTERNATIONAL_ALERT_OVERRIDE`) represent **operational decision support recommendations**.

- ML predictions indicate statistical probabilities based on synthetic datasets and historical indicators.
- Predictions do **NOT** constitute autonomous legal proof of criminal activity.

---

## 2. Human Authorization Mandate

1. **Patrol Dispatches**: PCR patrol unit dispatches must be authorized by an authorized police dispatcher or officer.
2. **Account Freezing**: Bank step-up authentication or debit freezing must be authorized by bank SOC analysts or compliance officers.
3. **Legal Dossier Submission**: Compiled PDFs and SHA-256 evidence hashes serve as standardized evidentiary packages for court submission, requiring signing by an issuing officer.

---

## 3. Data Provenance & Synthetic Disclaimers

All demonstration data, node coordinates, transaction chains, and synthetic user profiles are labeled with `DATA_PROVENANCE_SYNTHETIC` in API responses to prevent confusing prototype demonstrations with actual operational law enforcement telemetry.
